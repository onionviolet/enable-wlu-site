← Left · Done · Summer 2026

Hand one

Printed
SLS nylon, white PLA cuff
Problem
Powder stuck in the tendon channels. The first cuff was nylon too, and it snapped when we tried to shape it in hot water. Hot-water forming is meant for PLA; nylon barely softens at that temperature.
Fix
Cut a new channel through the palm with a Dremel. Reprinted the cuff in PLA, which shaped cleanly.

Questions

What is e-NABLE?

A global volunteer community that shares free designs for 3D-printed hands and arms. Learn more at Enabling the Future.

Do I need to know CAD or 3D printing?

No. We'll show you. Photographers, writers and organizers are useful too.

Can I request a hand here?

Not yet. Start with the e-NABLE Hub, which connects people with volunteers.
